The two magnitude columns are means of absolute shifts, averaged within issue and then over the 19 issues, with $t$-intervals on the issue clusters ($\mathrm{df}=18$). $\beta$ is the transmission slope of written on predicted shift, estimated at the issue level with a fixed effect per model, so it is a within-model rate; the reference of interest is $\beta = 1$, one unit of predicted shift becoming one unit of written shift, and $q$ is the Benjamini--Hochberg adjusted $p$ for $\beta \neq 1$ over this table's family of seven tests.
